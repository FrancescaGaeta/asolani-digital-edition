This interface presents manuscript Q, the 1505 edition, and the 1553 redaction in parallel. The alignment is based on TEI correspondence markers and preserves both parallel and non-parallel structures.

Editorial rationale

The witnesses are treated as autonomous textual systems. The alignment is derived from encoded correspondences (@corresp) in the TEI file. Where textual segments correspond, they are displayed in parallel; where they diverge, empty cells preserve structural asymmetry.
