Synoptic View

This prototype presents manuscript Q, the 1505 edition, and the 1553 redaction in parallel, making visible both textual correspondence and structural divergence.

Editorial rationale

The witnesses are treated as autonomous texts. Where they can be aligned, the interface supports direct comparison; where they diverge, the synoptic view preserves the non-parallel structure.
