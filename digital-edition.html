Markup

The project is encoded in TEI P5. The XML file records the textual witnesses, the structure of prose and verse sections, and the system of variation among manuscript Q, the 1505 edition, and the 1553 redaction.

The markup distinguishes between two editorial goals: first, a reading text based on the 1553 witness; second, a comparative representation of the three witnesses as autonomous textual forms.

Implementation choices

The project combines different presentation modes. The main edition is centred on the 1553 text and its variants, while the synoptic view displays the three witnesses in parallel.

This follows a logic similar to the kind of custom DSE model shown in the Bembo mockup you linked, where the interface separates reading mode, comparison mode, and outputs derived from the XML/TEI source. The same mockup also explains that prototype HTML views may be manually built first and later generated automatically from TEI through XSLT and JavaScript. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

For your project, this means: one TEI source, multiple views, and working prototypes embedded in the website.
