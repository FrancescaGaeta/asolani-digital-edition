Pietro Bembo

Bembo was a Venetian scholar, poet, literary theorist, and cardinal. He played a decisive role in the early sixteenth-century Italian language question by proposing Petrarch as a model for poetry and Boccaccio for prose.

Gli Asolani

Gli Asolani is a treatise in three books, written in dialogue form between 1497 and 1502. It discusses the nature of love in a structure combining prose and verse.

Princeps 1505

The 1505 edition is the princeps of the work and represents a crucial stage in its textual history.

Witness Q

Witness Q is particularly important in reconstructing the earlier textual state of the work.
