Pietro Bembo

Pietro Bembo occupies a central place in the literary and linguistic culture of the Italian Renaissance. His works shaped both the development of vernacular prose and the codification of literary language in the sixteenth century.

In the case of Gli Asolani, Bembo’s authorial practice is especially important because the work survives in witnesses that document different stages of composition, revision, and publication. This makes the text particularly suitable for a digital scholarly edition focused on textual development.

Gli Asolani

Gli Asolani is a prose dialogue centred on love and framed within the courtly setting of Asolo. The work combines literary refinement, rhetorical control, and a carefully structured alternation between prose and verse.

For the purposes of this project, the interest of the work lies not only in its literary value, but also in its textual history. The selected witnesses preserve significant macro-textual and micro-textual variation, making visible a process of authorial reworking across time.

Giorgio Dilemmi’s critical edition

The digital project is based on the critical edition prepared by Giorgio Dilemmi. That edition provides the philological foundation for the present work and makes it possible to study the relationships between manuscript Q, the 1505 printed witness, and the 1553 redaction.

In the digital environment, the critical edition is not simply reproduced. Instead, it is used as a starting point for modelling the witnesses, aligning comparable segments, and creating interfaces that support both reading and comparison.
