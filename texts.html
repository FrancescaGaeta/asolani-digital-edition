Pietro Bembo

  • 1470–1547
  • Bembo was a Venetian scholar, poet, literary theorist and cardinal.
  • He moved between Venice, Messina, Ferrara, Urbino, Rome and Padua.
  • He played a decisive role in the sixteenth-century Italian language question by proposing Petrarch as a model for poetry and Boccaccio for prose.
  • He supported this idea through a precise editorial programme that made Trecento classics more accessible.

Gli Asolani

The work

  • Gli Asolani is a treatise in three books, written in dialogue form between 1497 and 1502.
  • It discusses love through a prose-and-verse structure framed by a narrative setting inspired by Boccaccio.
  • The action unfolds over three days of celebrations in the gardens of the court of Caterina Cornaro at Asolo.

The content

  • Book I – Perottino: the unhappy lover speaks about the pain of love.
  • Book II – Gismondo: the happy lover illustrates the positive dimension of love.
  • Book III – Lavinello: he refutes the previous arguments and develops a Platonic and then ascetic vision of love.
