The project

This digital scholarly edition explores Gli Asolani through the comparison of manuscript Q, the 1505 printed edition, and the 1553 redaction.

The website is organised into a textual section devoted to author, work, and critical edition, and a digital section devoted to TEI markup, implementation choices, and working prototypes.
