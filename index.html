The Asolani by Pietro Bembo: a Digital Scholarly Edition

This project presents a digital scholarly edition of Gli Asolani by Pietro Bembo, based on Giorgio Dilemmi’s critical edition and focused on the comparison between manuscript Q, the 1505 printed edition, and the 1553 revised redaction.

End-of-course project for Digital Scholarly Editing · Francesca Gaeta · 2026

The website is organised into two main sections. The first is devoted to the texts: author, work, and critical edition. The second is devoted to the digital dimension: TEI markup, implementation choices, and working prototypes.
