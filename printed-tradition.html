Edition and prototypes

This section gathers the main working environments of the project: the edition based on the 1553 text and the synoptic comparison of the three witnesses.

Main edition

The main digital edition is based on the 1553 witness, which functions as the central reading text. Variants from Q and 1505 are encoded in the TEI source and can be represented through the dedicated interface.

Synoptic comparison

The synoptic prototype allows the three witnesses to be read side by side. Comparable segments are aligned, while structural divergences are preserved and made visible rather than normalised.
