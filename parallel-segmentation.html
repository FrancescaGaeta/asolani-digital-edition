Parallel Segmentation

Method and Alignment

Parallel segmentation is the editorial method used to align corresponding portions of text across different witnesses of Gli Asolani. Rather than treating each edition as an isolated object, the text is divided into comparable segments, allowing direct comparison of linguistic, structural, and editorial variations.

This approach is particularly useful for analysing Bembo’s progressive revision of the work, especially in light of his linguistic theory as formulated in the Prose della volgar lingua.

Method

Segmentation

The text is divided into meaningful units (paragraphs or sentences), each assigned a unique identifier.

Alignment

Corresponding segments from different editions are aligned to allow direct comparison.

Encoding

TEI-XML markup is used to encode structural divisions and relationships between segments.

Comparison

Variants, omissions, and additions are analysed across witnesses within a shared framework.

Visualization (EVT)

The encoded text is visualised through EVT (Edition Visualization Technology), which allows users to explore aligned segments dynamically. Readers can switch between witnesses, highlight variants, and observe how the text evolves across different editions.
