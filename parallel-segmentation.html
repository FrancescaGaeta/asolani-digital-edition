Critical View

EVT Integration

Method

Once the TEI file was completed, the encoded text was visualized through EVT (Edition Visualization Technology), an open-source web application specifically designed for the display and exploration of digital scholarly editions encoded in TEI XML.

EVT allows editors to transform structured TEI data into an interactive reading environment, supporting features such as apparatus visualization and multi-witness comparison without requiring complex backend infrastructures.

The encoding was developed in accordance with the use of parallel segmentation, which is fully supported by the platform. EVT dynamically aligns corresponding textual segments and manages the relationship between the base text and its variants in a coherent and readable way.

In the interface below, the 1553 is represented as the primary reading text. Users can interact directly with it: by clicking on a highlighted passage and opening the Info aggiuntive panel, the corresponding readings from the 1505 edition and manuscript Q can be displayed together with their witness information, allowing the three textual stages to be compared without interrupting the reading of the 1553 text. The interface also makes textual omissions immediately visible. When a passage has no textual content in the 1553 redaction but is present in one or both of the other witnesses, EVT displays a highlighted empty space in the base text. This visual marker can be selected like any other variant locus, giving access to the corresponding readings through the Additional information panel. Absence is therefore represented as meaningful textual information rather than remaining invisible within the interface.

This interactive mechanism reflects and enhances the underlying TEI structure. The apparatus is not presented as a static system, but as a dynamic and user-driven tool, enabling readers to explore textual variation progressively. In this way, EVT not only visualizes the encoded data but also translates editorial choices into an intuitive reading experience, making the comparison between witnesses more transparent and effective for both specialist and non-specialist users.
