Critical View

EVT Integration

Method

Once the TEI file was completed, the encoded text was visualized through EVT (Edition Visualization Technology), an open-source web application specifically designed for the display and exploration of digital scholarly editions encoded in TEI XML.

EVT allows editors to transform structured TEI data into an interactive reading environment, supporting features such as synchronized text navigation, apparatus visualization, and multi-witness comparison without requiring complex backend infrastructures.

The encoding was developed in accordance with the use of parallel segmentation, which is fully supported by the platform. EVT dynamically aligns corresponding textual segments and manages the relationship between the base text and its variants in a coherent and readable way.

In the interface below, the 1553 is represented as the primary reading text. Users can interact directly with it: by clicking on a lemma, the critical apparatus is activated and displayed in a dedicated section below the main text. This apparatus presents the variant readings from the other witnesses (the 1505 edition and manuscript Q), clearly identifying their sources and allowing for immediate comparison.

This interactive mechanism reflects and enhances the underlying TEI structure. The apparatus is not presented as a static, traditional note system, but as a dynamic and user-driven tool, enabling readers to explore textual variation progressively. In this way, EVT not only visualizes the encoded data but also translates editorial choices—such as parallel segmentation—into an accessible and intuitive reading experience, making the comparison between witnesses more transparent and effective for both specialist and non-specialist users.
